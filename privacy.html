Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kiko's Puzzle: Block Adventure (“the Game”), developed by Harun Yavuz (Yavuz Studio, “we”), handles information when you play.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress on your device only: levels and stars, coins, items and outfits, lives, tools, settings and the nickname you choose. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive this information. It is deleted when you delete the Game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (for example, which ads were shown or tapped)
  • Diagnostic and performance data (for example, crash logs)

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you don't allow tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Game Center

If the Game offers Apple Game Center features (such as leaderboards) and you choose to use them, your Game Center nickname and scores are processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated for general audiences.

5. Your choices

  • Turn off ad tracking in your device settings at any time.
  • Delete the Game to remove all locally saved data.
  • Contact us with any privacy question or request.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
